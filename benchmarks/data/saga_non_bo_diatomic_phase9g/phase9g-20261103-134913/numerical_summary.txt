Conclusion
----------
Phase 9g passes. Length- and velocity-gauge discrepancies in the physical
observables decrease strongly with l_max. At l_max=8, the remaining gauge
differences are separated from the measured time-step errors and are
consistent with finite angular and radial representation errors.

The midpoint propagation displays the expected time-step convergence.
Time steps 0.005 and 0.0025 provide reference-quality l_max=8 results.
The dt=0.02 l_max=8 runs should not be used as performance references because
they triggered significantly more expensive Krylov refinement.
